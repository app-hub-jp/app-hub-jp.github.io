Privacy Policy

English

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mitsuru Shimozaki ("we" or "us") handles information about users of the Spot app ("the App").

1. Operator

The App is operated by Mitsuru Shimozaki. We will provide our address on request.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide the App.

(1) Information you provide

  • Account: an identifier from Sign in with Apple. Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication by Google. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • Profile: your name, username, photo, bio, country, category, and birth year if you enter it.
  • Submissions: the lift, weight, body weight, comment, video, and date of each submission, along with its review result and any message from the reviewer.
  • Activity: lifters you follow or block, and reports you send, including what you write in them.
  • Purchases: records of credit purchases that we receive from Apple, and your credit balance. We do not receive your payment card or other payment details.
  • Inquiries: your email address and the content of your message when you contact us.

(2) Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: a token for your device and your notification settings, if you allow notifications.
  • Access records: records of the App's communication with our servers, such as IP address, approximate location (country, region, and city), and device type.
  • Device check: to give the sign-up bonus only once per device, we use Apple's DeviceCheck. It records only a marker for the device and does not identify you.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the App, including profiles, rankings, follows, and the notifications you allow
  • Review submissions and check profile photos
  • Respond to reports and prevent misuse
  • Add purchased credits
  • Fix problems and keep the App secure
  • Answer your inquiries

We do not sell your information. The App shows no ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Information Shown to Others

The following information is public in the App and can be seen by anyone, including people without an account:

  • Your profile (name, username, photo, bio, country, category, and age if you entered your birth year)
  • Your approved lifts, their comments, and your rank history

Your body weight is shown only if you allow it. Videos are never public. Only you and our reviewers can watch them.

5. Service Providers

We entrust information to the following providers only as needed to provide the App. They handle it only for the services we use and protect it at a level equal to this policy.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (servers, and storage of data, videos, and photos; United States)
  • Google LLC (sign-in through Firebase Authentication)
  • Apple Inc. (sign-in, purchases, notifications, and DeviceCheck)

The App's data is stored on servers in the United States.

6. Retention

  • Videos are deleted a limited time after review.
  • Access records are deleted after a few days.
  • Inquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond.
  • Other information is kept until your account is deleted.

7. Security

We protect information against leaks and misuse by encrypting communication, restricting access to our admin tools, and using temporary links to show videos. Because information is stored in the United States, we take these measures with an understanding of that country's privacy laws.

8. Deleting Your Account and Information

You can delete your account at any time from Delete account in the App's Settings. This deletes your profile, lifts, videos, photo, follows, blocks, and credits. Reports you sent are kept without information that identifies you. Apple keeps its own records of your purchases.

9. Your Requests

You can ask us by email for a copy of your information, or to correct, delete, or stop using it. We will respond promptly after confirming that the request comes from you. You can also contact us at the same address with any complaint about how we handle information.

10. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may change this policy when needed. When we do, we will update this page and its date. We will also let you know in the App about important changes.

12. Contact

supporthubjp+spot@gmail.com
